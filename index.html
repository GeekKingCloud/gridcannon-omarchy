YOUR PLOYS

Field guide

Play a card opposite a royal. The two cards between become your payload. Their combined matching value must meet the royal’s health.

Your trigger card never contributes damage. Shots don’t consume cards. A corner can fire in two directions.

Highlighted slots are legal. Royal badges show current payload / health. Hover or focus a grid slot for a shot preview.

1–9 select grid positions in reading order. Use Tab and Enter for all other controls.

The art of the shot

Defeat all twelve royals around the grid. Numbers go on equal or lower numbers, or empty spaces. Royals go beside the highest eligible card of their suit, then colour, then any suit. You choose ties.

Fire across the grid

Place at the far end of a row or column. Only the two intervening cards attack: Jacks need 11 in any suit; Queens need 12 in their colour; Kings need 13 in their suit. Armour increases that health. Defeated royals still occupy their slots.

Revised · creator’s version 2

Start with nine number cards. You may replace one before play. Aces and jokers are banked, never placed on the grid. Spend an ace to extract a whole stack to the bottom of the deck; spend a joker to move one top card to a legal destination, firing as normal.

A blocked draw with no ploys must become armour on the most similar living royal (lowest rank of matching suit, then colour, then any). Health of 20+, or 19+ for a King, ends the game. An empty deck with no ploys also ends the game. Your winning score is unspent ploys.

Classic · creator’s original version 1

Start with an empty centre. Aces and jokers can be placed anywhere: recycle the old stack under the deck and leave the special card on top. A blocked draw can become armour, or go to shame while you recycle a stack. When the deck is empty, choose a stack: its top card goes to shame and the rest becomes the new deck. Aim for as little shame as possible.

Neither version loses merely because one shot fails. When no living royal remains, drawing cycles past non-royals and returns them under the deck.

Digital conventions: only unoccupied border slots participate in royal placement. Recycled stacks keep bottom-to-top order. A replacement deal banks specials and queues royals until a number fills the slot. See README for source ambiguities; these are not additional official rules.
